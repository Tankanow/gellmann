The response must open with a short briefing on the DOMAIN (Euclidean
compass-and-straightedge construction), not with a judgment of the AI answer.

PASS if the response contains a few short declarative sentences teaching the
subject itself — e.g. that a construction proves only what its theorem
proves, that two equal circles centered at the endpoints cross at two points and the
line through them bisects the segment at right angles (Euclid I.10 bisects;
the right angle follows from congruent triangles), and that how a drawing
*looks* is not part of a proof. The
briefing should be roughly 3-5 sentences and should be understandable to a
parent who does not know geometry.

FAIL if the response instead leads with an evaluation of the AI answer
("this is wrong because...", "the response misread the diagram", numbered
findings, a corrected explanation presented as authoritative), or if it
gives no domain teaching at all and only routes.
