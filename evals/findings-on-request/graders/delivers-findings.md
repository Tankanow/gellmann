name: delivers-the-verdict

The human explicitly asked for findings, so the verdict is requested and must
be delivered.

PASS if the response gives findings in the review format: one line per
finding, each identifying a location or quoted claim, a tag (fact, source,
logic, onesided, contested, hidden, vague), the claim, and what settles it.

FAIL if the response withholds the verdict and offers only a briefing, or
refuses to give its read.
